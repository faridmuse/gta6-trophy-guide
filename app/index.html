    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough — every trophy is offline single-player, and there are no online trophies. Work the stages in order; the story first, then the side content, then the clean-up. The only thing to do early is the meditation trophy (see Missables). Using cheats disables trophies, so leave them off for the whole run.

      Roadmap built from the official descriptions: which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. Estimated time: the story alone runs about 60 hours (per reviews cited by Planet GTA 6); the platinum is estimated at around 200 hours, mostly because of 100% completion.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable by any guide — and even that one is not confirmed. Everything else can be finished after the story.

      Why so few?

      Story trophies unlock automatically when their chapter or mission ends, and they cannot be skipped. Activities, races, robberies, collectibles and money trophies all stay open in free roam after the story, and the 100% completion trophy is pure post-game clean-up. The honest state of knowledge, as of the game launching on 19 November 2026: Planet GTA 6 says there is no reliable list of missable trophies yet, while PowerPyx and GTABase flag one — meditation. This guide flags that one and says plainly where the evidence ends.

      Safe habits for a trophy run

      • Keep a few manual saves before the big missions (Planet GTA 6's standing advice while no reliable missables list exists).
      • Meditate at every spot the moment you find it — do not leave Positive Mental Attitude for the post-game.
      • Start every stranger strand as soon as it appears on the map, so none is left to find later.
      • Do not use cheats: they disable trophies and saving (PowerPyx, via Planet GTA 6).
      • Finish open side content before the final mission — it is the natural point of no return.

      Sources & method

      Every trophy name, description and grade is the official PlayStation 5 list as Exophase and PSNProfiles published it before this guide was built (frozen 18 November 2026): 55 trophies, 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden. The guides are this entry's own words, built from those official descriptions. Where a community guide is the source of a claim, it is named beside that trophy's guide — a source is cited, never copied.

      What each guide says about its own certainty

      • From the description alone: most guides follow directly from what the official description asks — a count, a place, a mission name. No in-game check was possible; the game is a day old.
      • Flagged, not confirmed: the one possibly missable trophy and the cheat warning come from PowerPyx / GTABase as reported by EGamers and GTAScoop, and Planet GTA 6 notes no reliable missables list exists yet.
      • Not confirmed in the game: the five cryptic trophies (Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Let's Go Bowling) give no actionable instruction in their descriptions, so their guides say what the description suggests and mark the method unconfirmed rather than inventing steps.
